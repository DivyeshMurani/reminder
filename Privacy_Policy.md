## 2. Information Collection and Use

### A. Local Reminder Data
All reminders, subtasks, due dates, priorities, notes, and task completion statuses created within the app are stored **locally on your device** using Apple's SwiftData storage framework. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your local reminder content to any external servers operated by us.

### B. Local Notifications
The app uses Apple's local notification framework (`UNUserNotificationCenter`) to send scheduled reminder alerts and early notifications to your device. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use remote push notification servers to process or deliver your reminder alerts.

### C. Apple iCloud & CloudKit Sync (Optional / Premium Feature)
If you subscribe to Premium and choose to use the iCloud Backup & Restore feature:
- Encrypted backup snapshots of your reminders are stored directly in your personal **Apple iCloud private database** (`CloudKit`).
- We (the developer) do NOT have access to your personal iCloud database, backup files, or reminder content.
- Your iCloud backup data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

### D. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
In-App Purchases (Monthly and Yearly Premium Subscriptions) are processed directly by Apple via **StoreKit 2**.
- We do NOT collect, store, or process any payment, credit card, or billing information.
- All transactions, payment processing, and subscription renewals are managed securely by Apple.
