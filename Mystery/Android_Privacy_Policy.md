# Privacy Policy

**Mystery Macarons** · *Effective date: October 26, 2026*

This policy explains what information is handled when you play **Mystery Macarons** (the "Game"), made by **Divyesh Murani** ("we"). The short version: **we do not collect your name, email, contacts or any other personal details.** The Game shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for game statistics and crash reports. Both handle some device information as described below.

---

## Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or any account, and we have no servers of our own. The only information we receive is the game statistics and crash reports described under *Game statistics and crash reports* below.

---

## Information Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (levels completed, daily puzzle streak), your settings (sound, music, vibration, colorblind mode, reduce motion), how many power-ups you have left, simple ad counters (for example how many free hints you used today) and whether you bought Remove Ads are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

---

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

- Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), when permitted;
- Your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- Device and game information, such as device model, operating system and game version;
- Information about your interaction with ads.

Ads are personalized only where you allow it:

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where it is required, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Game under *Settings › Ad privacy choices*.
- On iPhone and iPad, the Game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your IDFA is not used. You can change this in *iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking*.
- On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (*Settings › Google › Ads* or *Settings › Privacy › Ads*, depending on your device).

Google's use of this information is described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## Game Statistics and Crash Reports (Google Firebase)

To find levels that are too hard and to fix bugs, the Game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They are only switched on where the consent rules above allow ads, and they send:

- **Game events:** a level started, completed or left, with the level, number of moves and time taken; when a hint, undo, extra box or power-up is used; whether a rewarded ad was watched; whether Remove Ads was bought or restored; whether a game update or rating prompt was shown;
- A random game installation identifier, your device's advertising identifier where permitted, device model, operating system, game version, language and approximate country;
- **Crash reports:** technical details of errors in the Game, such as the device state and the part of the code where the error happened.

This information is not linked to your name or any account, is not sold, and is used only to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. Firebase keeps it only for a limited time, under Google's retention settings. See [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

---

## In-Game Purchase

The Game offers one optional purchase, *Remove Ads*. It is sold and paid for through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. The Game only learns from the store whether Remove Ads was bought, and keeps that on your device so ads stay off. If analytics is on (see above), the Game also records whether a purchase or restore succeeded. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover the payment itself.

---

## Game Updates and Ratings

The Game may ask Google Play or the App Store whether a newer version is available, and may show the store's own dialog asking you to rate the Game. These are handled by Google and Apple; we do not receive your rating, review or account details. Their privacy policies apply.

---

## Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## Network Access

The game works fully offline. The Game only uses the internet to load ads and the consent form, to send the game statistics and crash reports described above, and to talk to Google Play or the App Store about the Remove Ads purchase, game updates and ratings.
