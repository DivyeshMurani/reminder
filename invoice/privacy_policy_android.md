# Privacy Policy (Android)

*Effective Date: August 11, 2026*

This Privacy Policy governs the use of **Invoice Generator** ("App") on Android devices, developed by **Divyesh Murani** ("Developer"). Our commitment is to provide a secure and private environment for creating and managing your professional business invoices, client lists, and items catalog.

---

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

The App is designed as an offline-first tool. Your business records and personal data remain under your direct control on your physical device.

### A. Invoice, Business Profile and Customer Information
The App allows you to input invoice details, including company names, addresses, contacts, client names, mobile numbers, items, tax percentages, and totals. This data is stored **locally** on your device's secure storage. We do not collect, upload, or transmit this information to any external servers.

### B. Backup & Restore Services
The App provides an optional manual backup and restore feature allowing you to export your data. These files are saved directly to your device's local storage, from where you can manually share or backup to your cloud storage. We do not automatically collect or upload your backups.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Integrations

To support the free version of the App, monitor performance, and ensure software stability, the App integrates standard Google Play and Firebase SDKs:

- **Google AdMob:** Displays advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and interaction data to serve and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and report ad metrics.
- **Firebase Analytics:** Collects anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (such as screen view events, feature interactions, and session durations) to help us analyze App performance and improve the user interface. No personally identifiable invoice, business profile, or customer data is ever transmitted.
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** Collects anonymous crash logs, stack traces, operating system versions, and hardware device models when an error occurs to assist the Developer in diagnosing bugs and maintaining application stability.

---

## 3. In-App Subscriptions and Billing

The App offers optional Pro Member auto-renewable subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly). All financial transactions and payment processing are handled directly and securely by **Google Play Store Billing**. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card or financial info.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off in Google Play Store settings at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can manage or cancel your active subscriptions at any time via your Google Play account settings.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We **do not sell**, trade, rent, or share your business profiles, customer details, or invoice histories with any third parties. Your records remain private on your device.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Security

Because your data is stored locally, you are in control of its retention. Deleting the App will permanently delete all local databases and records from your device. If you have exported manual backups, they remain under your control in your local storage files.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business owners and general audiences, and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or via the Google Play Store listing.
